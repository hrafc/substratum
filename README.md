# Substratum

SUBSTRATUM
Deep Subterranean Horror • First-Person Survival

OVERVIEW
Step into the suffocating darkness of Sector 4 in SUBSTRATUM, an intense first-person survival horror experience set deep within an abandoned underground metro bunker complex. Something far worse than structural failure has awakened in the subterranean grid. As a lone survivor deployed into pitch-black concrete labyrinths, your objective is clear: navigate the damp, industrial corridors, gather lost research audio logs, decipher handwritten high-security vault codes, and unlock the heavy blast doors before the entity hunting you strikes from the shadows.

THE THREAT
You are not alone in the subterranean depths. Stalking the narrow sub-levels is a monstrous, ash-grey entity—a horrific biological mutation featuring long heavy limbs, jagged spine spikes, glowing orange eyes, and an exposed ribcage radiating a sickening crimson pulse. It reacts to your every movement, tracking footfalls and lock mechanisms along the concrete floor. When it picks up your trail, intense chase music ignites, the corridors echo with guttural zombie-like growls, and your vision shakes to the violent, rapid pulse of your own panicking heartbeat.

KEY FEATURES

Suffocating Survival Horror: Explore expanding multi-tier sub-level grids with strictly managed stamina and a dim, flickering flashlight. Every sprint consumes critical energy, leaving you exhausted when you need speed the most.

Investigative Vault Mechanics: Scour maintenance tunnels for hidden audio logs and field reports detailing the bunker's final hours. Hunt down handwritten clipboard access codes to breach heavy security vaults containing master escape keys.

Procedural Horror Audio Design: Built for headphones, SUBSTRATUM utilizes spatial positional audio, low-frequency sub-drones, wet dragging footstep cues, and terrifying vocal growls that keep you hyper-aware of your surroundings.

Pulse-Synchronized Visuals & Proximity Tracking: Keep an eye on your proximity signal meter to locate key objectives, while dynamic camera distortion, pulse-synced camera shaking, and atmospheric vignette shifts signal the creature’s dangerously close presence.

Relentless Pursuit AI: The creature navigates maze networks with unyielding pathfinding. Once the final vault lock turns, all stealth vanishes—a desperate sprint for the east blast door is your only hope for survival.

Do you have what it takes to decipher the codes and escape the dark, or will your signal flatline inside the subterranean grid? Put on your headphones, keep your flashlight ready, and initialize Protocol 8.
